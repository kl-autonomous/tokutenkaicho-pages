Tokutenkai-cho Support

Tokutenkai-cho (特典会帳) is a logbook for underground-idol fans: events, cheki and spending. It needs iOS 26 or later.

How do I log a cheki?

Tap + at the bottom, pick the idol, add the count, and tap Save. A new event is made for you; tap More… to change its date, ticket or anything else.

How do I back up?

Settings → Export backup saves one file to Files, a computer or wherever you like. Settings → Import backup brings it back. The app also keeps a daily copy on the device.

Moving to a new phone

On the old phone, open Settings → Sync and wait until it shows when it last synced, with nothing left to upload. On the new phone, signed in to the same Apple Account, open the app, start, and switch sync on: your records and photos come back from iCloud. Importing an exported backup works too.

Coming from the web version

Export a file from the web version, then use Settings → Import from the web version in the app.

Using it on an iPhone and a Mac

Sign each device in to the same Apple Account, and switch sync on in the app's Settings → Sync on each one. Your records and photos then sync between them. A Mac needs Apple silicon.

Privacy

Your records stay on your devices and in your own iCloud. No account, no tracking, no ads. See the privacy policy.
