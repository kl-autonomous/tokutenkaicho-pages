Tokutenkai-cho Privacy Policy

Updated October 26, 2026

Tokutenkai-cho (特典会帳) is an iPhone app for logging the events you go to, the cheki you take and what you spend. We collect no data about you.

What we collect

Nothing. There's no account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no server of our own. We can't see your records.

Two things can reach us from outside the app. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us crash reports, without your name or records. If you email us, we get your email address and message, used only to answer you.

Where your records are

  • On your device. Every record, photo and video is stored on your iPhone (or Mac).
  • In your own iCloud, if you switch it on in the app's Settings → Sync. Your records, photos and videos are kept in the private iCloud space of your Apple Account, which only you can read; we can't. What goes to iCloud is encrypted.
  • In backup files you export, wherever you put them.

Photos, videos, the camera and the photo library

When you take or choose a photo of a cheki, the app keeps its own copy, without the place it was taken. The same goes for a video you choose from your library: the app keeps its own copy, without the place it was taken. The camera is used only when you take a photo. The photo library is used only when you tap "View original" or "Save to Photos" (a photo or a video), and only after you allow it. A photo or video you share goes where you send it. You can change either in the iPhone's Settings at any time.

Links

Links you add, such as a ticket link, open in Safari; those websites have their own policies.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes the records on that device. For the copy in iCloud: in the iPhone's Settings, open your Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage, choose Tokutenkai-cho and delete its data. You can export a backup from the app first.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If anything changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
